There is one licence, the Possession and Acquisition Licence (PAL). What it covers depends on the safety courses you pass. People call the version that includes restricted firearms an "RPAL".

## Non-restricted PAL

- **Course:** the Canadian Firearms Safety Course (CFSC).
- **Covers:** non-restricted firearms, which are most hunting rifles and shotguns.
- **Who can take it:** anyone, including minors.

## Restricted PAL (RPAL)

- **Courses:** both the CFSC and the Canadian Restricted Firearms Safety Course (CRFSC).
- **Covers:** non-restricted and restricted firearms. Most handguns are restricted.
- **Who can take it:** the CRFSC is for people aged 18 and over.
- **Order:** many course providers require you to pass the CFSC before the CRFSC. You can often book both together and take them back to back.

## Which one should you get?

- **Hunting only:** the CFSC is enough for the licence. Hunting also needs your province's hunter education and a hunting licence. The handbook says you cannot hunt with a restricted firearm in Canada.
- **Target shooting with handguns:** you need both courses. A restricted firearm can only be loaded and fired where it is lawful to do so, such as an approved range.
- **Not sure yet:** taking both at once is usually cheaper and faster than coming back for the CRFSC later. Your licence goes through one application either way.

## Practise for both

This app has separate practice exams for the CFSC and the CRFSC. The CRFSC adds revolvers, semi-automatic pistols, handgun shooting technique, and the storage and transport rules for restricted firearms.

Sources: RCMP Canadian Firearms Program, safety course pages and PAL application information sheet (RCMP 5592); student handbook, 2014, pages 220 and 322.
