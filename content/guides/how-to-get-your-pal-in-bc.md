Getting a PAL in British Columbia takes three steps: pass the course, apply to the RCMP Canadian Firearms Program, and wait for your card. Most of the time is the wait.

## 1. Pass the course

- **Non-restricted licence:** pass the Canadian Firearms Safety Course (CFSC).
- **Restricted licence (RPAL):** pass the CFSC and the Canadian Restricted Firearms Safety Course (CRFSC). Many BC instructors run the two back to back.
- **Finding a course:** the RCMP's "Find an instructor" page lists certified instructors by province. Many courses are also offered through gun clubs and outdoor stores.
- **The tests:** each course ends with a written test and a practical handling test. You need 80% on each.

## 2. Apply for your licence

- **Online:** first-time applicants who live in Canada can apply online through a MyCFP account. Sign-up uses a GCKey or a Sign-in Partner (your bank).
- **By mail:** use form RCMP 5592, the application for a Possession and Acquisition Licence for people 18 and over.
- **What you need:** your course report, a photo that meets the RCMP photo rules, references, and the fee. The fee changes every year, so check the current amount on the RCMP site.

## 3. Wait for your card

- By law, a first licence can't be issued until at least 28 days after the application.
- Many applications take longer. This app's wait-time page shows what BC applicants report, and has a tracker for your own application.
- If something is missing, the Canadian Firearms Program will contact you or your references. Answering quickly is the best way to avoid a long wait.

## While you wait

- **Planning to hunt?** A PAL alone doesn't let you hunt in BC. You also need CORE, BC's hunter education course, and a hunting licence. The guide "How to get a hunting licence in BC" walks through it.
- **Want to shoot at a range?** Many BC ranges are run by clubs, so check a club's membership and orientation requirements before you go. Shootable, which shows where you can legally shoot in BC, is coming soon.

Sources: RCMP Canadian Firearms Program, "Apply for a firearms licence", "Firearms forms" (RCMP 5592) and "Find an instructor"; student handbook, 2014.
