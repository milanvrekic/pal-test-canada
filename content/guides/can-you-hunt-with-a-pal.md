title: Can you hunt with just a PAL?
description: Why a firearms licence alone does not let you hunt, and what else you need in British Columbia and Nova Scotia.

---
No. A PAL lets you possess and buy firearms. Hunting is regulated by your province, and every province has its own requirements on top of the firearms licence. For first-time hunters these usually include a hunter education course and a hunting licence.

## British Columbia

1. **Pass CORE.** The Conservation and Outdoor Recreation Education program is BC's hunter education course, run by the BC Wildlife Federation. It covers safe firearms handling, conservation, ethics, the law, first aid and survival, and wildlife identification. You finish with an exam in person with a CORE examiner.
2. **Get your hunting credentials.** Passing CORE adds hunting credentials to your Fish and Wildlife ID (FWID) profile.
3. **Buy a hunting licence,** plus species licences for the game you will hunt. You need your FWID or hunter number and photo ID.

First-time adult hunters can also buy a one-time initiation licence and hunt with a licensed mentor before doing CORE.

## Nova Scotia

1. **Pass the CFSC.** It is the first part of Nova Scotia's hunter education.
2. **Pass the Nova Scotia Hunter Education course.** It covers wildlife management, ethics, landowner relations, the law, first aid and survival, and field techniques. It is available online.
3. **Get your Wildlife Resources Card** showing your firearm certification, then buy a hunting licence.

## Other provinces

Every province requires hunter education for new hunters, but the course and the steps differ. Check your provincial wildlife department before you book.

## While you wait for your licence

Hunter education doesn't depend on your PAL arriving. Many people do both at the same time, so they are ready to hunt the season their licence comes. Once you are licensed, Huntable shows what is open in your zone in BC and Nova Scotia.
