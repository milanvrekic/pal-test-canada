Passing the course does not give you a licence. You still have to apply to the RCMP Canadian Firearms Program.

## The steps

1. **Get your course report.** Your instructor submits your results. You need proof of passing for the application.
2. **Complete the application.** New applicants use the Possession and Acquisition Licence application for individuals aged 18 and over. It asks for personal history, references and a photo.
3. **Submit it and pay the fee.** Fees change, so check the current amount on the RCMP site.
4. **Wait.** The law sets a minimum 28-day waiting period for anyone who does not already hold a licence. Many applications take longer.
5. **Receive your card.** The licence card arrives by mail after approval.

## What slows applications down

- Missing or unsigned sections.
- A photo that does not meet the specifications.
- References who cannot be reached.
- Personal history answers that need a follow-up review.
